# Client Contract Checklist

- [ ] Scope matches the proposal (programme, number of sessions, dates).
- [ ] Payment terms: 50% on signing, 50% before the Strategy session.
- [ ] Cancellation clause: sessions cancelled with under 48 hours' notice are charged.
- [ ] Data clause: we store intake answers for 12 months after the programme ends.
- [ ] Signed copy saved to the client folder before the Discovery Workshop is booked.
