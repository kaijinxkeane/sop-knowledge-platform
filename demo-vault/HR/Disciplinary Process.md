# Disciplinary Process (HR only)

1. Manager documents the concern with dates and facts.
2. HR holds an informal conversation within 5 working days.
3. If unresolved, a formal written warning is issued with a 30-day improvement plan.
4. Final review meeting with HR and the CEO.
