# Leave Policy

- Annual leave: 18 days per year, pro-rated in the first year.
- Apply in the HR system at least 2 weeks ahead for leave longer than 3 days.
- Trainers must not take leave on dates with booked client sessions unless cover is arranged.
- Medical leave: inform your manager before 9 am and submit the certificate within 2 days.
