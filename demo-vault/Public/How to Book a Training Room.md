# How to Book a Training Room

1. Open the shared "Rooms" calendar.
2. Create an event in the room's calendar with the programme name and trainer.
3. Large Room seats 12; Small Room seats 6. Book 30 minutes before and after for setup.
4. Cancel bookings you no longer need at least 24 hours ahead.
