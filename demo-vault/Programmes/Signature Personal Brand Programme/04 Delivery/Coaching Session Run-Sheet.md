# Coaching Session Run-Sheet

Fortnightly 45-minute coaching calls during weeks 5 to 12.

- [ ] Review last two weeks of posts and engagement before the call (10 minutes prep).
- [ ] Open with one win and one challenge from the client.
- [ ] Coach on one skill only per session (e.g. hooks, storytelling, commenting strategy).
- [ ] Agree two actions and log them in the client tracker within the same day.
- [ ] If the client misses two sessions in a row, escalate to the lead trainer.
