# Brand Pillars Workshop

A 2-hour, one-to-one session held 2 to 3 weeks after Discovery.

1. Review the client's top five values and the brand gaps from Discovery.
2. Apply the [[Brand Pillars Framework]] to turn them into **three brand pillars**, each with a
   one-sentence promise and two proof points.
3. Pressure-test each pillar with the "would a colleague recognise this?" question.
4. Write the pillars into the client's Brand Blueprint document and send it within 2 working days.

If the client cannot settle on three pillars, park the third and revisit it in the content stage.
