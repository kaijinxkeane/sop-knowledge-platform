# 90-Day Review

Held 90 days after the Discovery Workshop.

1. Re-run the "how others see you" section of the intake questionnaire with the same colleagues.
2. Compare results with the original answers and show the shift on one slide.
3. Ask for a testimonial only if the client rates the programme 8/10 or higher.
4. Offer the alumni community invitation and close the client folder after 30 days.
