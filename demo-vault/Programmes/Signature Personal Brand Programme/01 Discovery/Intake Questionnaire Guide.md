# Intake Questionnaire Guide

Every Signature client completes the intake questionnaire **at least 5 working days** before the
[[Discovery Workshop SOP|Discovery Workshop]].

- Send the questionnaire link from the shared delivery inbox, never from a personal address.
- The client nominates **3 to 5 colleagues** who each answer the "how others see you" section.
- Chase once after 3 days. If it is still missing 2 days before the workshop, tell the lead trainer,
  who decides whether to reschedule.
- Answers are stored in the client folder. Do not paste answers into chat tools or email threads.
