# Running the Discovery Workshop

The Discovery Workshop is the first live session of the [[Signature Personal Brand Programme]].
It lasts 3 hours and is run by one lead trainer and one assistant.

## 1. Before the session (T-5 working days)
1. Confirm the client has returned the [[Intake Questionnaire Guide|intake questionnaire]].
2. Book a training room (see [[How to Book a Training Room]]).
3. Print one [[Values Card Sort Worksheet.pdf|Values Card Sort worksheet]] per participant.
4. Load the ![[Brand Foundations.pptx]] deck on the room laptop and test the clicker.

## 2. Opening (20 minutes)
- Welcome, agenda and ground rules (slides 1-4 of the Brand Foundations deck).
- Run the **3-minute brand story** exercise: each participant tells the story of a moment
  they were proud of their work. The assistant notes recurring words on the whiteboard.

## 3. Values card sort (45 minutes)
- Participants work in **pairs**. Each person sorts the 40 value cards into
  "always me", "sometimes me" and "not me", then explains their top five to their partner.
- Time-box to 15 minutes per person plus 15 minutes of group debrief.
- Record the top five values on the worksheet; collect worksheets at the end.

## 4. Brand gap discussion (60 minutes)
- Compare how participants see themselves with the intake questionnaire answers from
  colleagues. Use slides 14-18. Keep the tone exploratory, not judgmental.

## 5. Close (15 minutes)
- Agree one action for the next two weeks. Book the [[Brand Pillars Workshop SOP|Strategy session]].
- Within 24 hours, the assistant uploads photos of the whiteboard to the client folder.
