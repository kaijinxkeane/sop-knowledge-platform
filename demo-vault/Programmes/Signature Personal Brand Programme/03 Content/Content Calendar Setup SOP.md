# Content Calendar Setup

1. Create a 12-week calendar from the "Content Calendar" template in [[SOP Template|Shared Templates]].
2. Assign each week to one brand pillar, rotating so no pillar is used twice in a row.
3. Plan **two posts per week**: one story post and one insight post.
4. Draft the first four posts with the client in a 60-minute working session, using the
   [[Voice and Story Method]] to keep the client's natural voice.
5. Agree who presses "publish": the client always publishes from their own account.
