# Executive Pre-Workshop Interview

A 30-minute call with each executive before the group workshop.

- Ask about the three situations where they most want to "show up better" (board meetings,
  town halls, media, client pitches).
- Ask how comfortable they are on camera, on a 1-5 scale.
- Note anything they do not want discussed in front of the group, and respect it.
- Share a summary with the lead trainer only; never with the executive's employer.
