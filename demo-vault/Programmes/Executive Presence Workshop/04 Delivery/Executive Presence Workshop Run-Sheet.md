# Executive Presence Workshop Run-Sheet

Full-day workshop for 6 to 10 executives. Deck: ![[Executive Presence.pptx]]

| Time | Segment | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| 09:00 | Arrival and coffee | Name cards, camera consent forms |
| 09:30 | Presence fundamentals | Slides 1-9 |
| 10:45 | Voice and pace drill | Pairs, 2-minute talks, record on phone |
| 12:30 | Lunch | |
| 13:30 | On-camera practice | One camera, one light; 3 takes per person |
| 15:30 | Playback and feedback | Feedback using "keep / try" format only |
| 16:45 | Personal commitment | Each executive writes one commitment card |

Participants who are uncomfortable on camera may do their first take audio-only.
