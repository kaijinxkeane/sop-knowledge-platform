# Voice and Story Method

How we help clients write in their own voice.

- Record the client talking about a topic for 5 minutes; draft from the transcript, not from scratch.
- Keep the client's own phrases; remove only filler words.
- Every story post follows **moment, tension, lesson, invitation**.
- Read the draft aloud with the client; anything they would not say out loud gets rewritten.
