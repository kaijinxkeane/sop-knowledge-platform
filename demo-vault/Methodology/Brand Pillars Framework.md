# Brand Pillars Framework (proprietary)

Our core method for turning values and reputation into three brand pillars.

1. **Signal:** list every value and strength that appears in at least two sources
   (self-view, colleague view, track record).
2. **Cluster:** group signals into themes; discard themes with only one source.
3. **Promise:** for each theme write "I help [who] to [outcome] by [how]".
4. **Proof:** attach two proof points (stories, results, credentials) to each promise.
5. **Select:** choose the three promises with the strongest proof and the clearest audience.

Scoring: each candidate pillar is scored 1-5 on distinctiveness, credibility and audience fit;
pillars below 9 total are rejected.
