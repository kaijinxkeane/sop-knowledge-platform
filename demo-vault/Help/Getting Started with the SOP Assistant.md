# Getting Started with the SOP Assistant

The SOP Assistant is where the company keeps its standard operating procedures (SOPs), training material and know-how. You can ask it questions, read and write notes, and see what you need to learn.

## Signing in

1. Open the link your administrator sent you and choose a password.
2. Set up two-factor sign-in: scan the code with an authenticator app on your phone, then type the 6-digit code it shows.
3. Read and accept the staff notice. It explains what the system records and for how long.

Each time you sign in you enter your email, your password and the current code from your authenticator app. If you lose your phone, ask an administrator to reset your two-factor sign-in; you set it up again the next time you sign in.

## Finding your way around

The menu at the top shows only what your role allows:

- **Home**: your required reading, onboarding paths, recently approved SOPs and anything waiting for you.
- **Ask**: ask a question and get an answer from approved SOPs, with sources.
- **Library**: browse and search every document you can open.
- **Write**: create and edit notes.
- **Review**: for space leads, approve changes and check AI label suggestions.
- **Retain**: for leads and HR, keep knowledge from getting lost.
- **Handovers** and **Projects**: hand work over to a colleague, or work with people from other departments.
- **Admin**: for administrators.

The bell shows your notifications, such as a change waiting for your review or a new item of required reading. Press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) anywhere to jump to a document by its title.

## Using it like an app

In Chrome or Edge, choose **Install app** next to Sign out. The SOP Assistant then opens in its own window from your dock or desktop and always shows the latest version.

> [!tip] Can't find something?
> If a document doesn't appear, you may not have access to it. Documents you can't open look exactly like documents that don't exist. Ask your space lead if you think you should see it.
