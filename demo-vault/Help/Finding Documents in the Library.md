# Finding Documents in the Library

The **Library** lists every document you are allowed to open: SOPs, guides, slide decks, worksheets and PDFs.

## Searching and browsing

- Type in the search box to find documents by their words, not only their titles.
- Switch the view to browse by space, by programme and stage, or by type.
- Press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) anywhere to jump straight to a document by its title.
- Select the star on a document to bookmark it. Bookmarks appear on Home.

## Reading a document

- Links to other notes open them directly. A link to a document you can't open shows as "not available".
- Slide decks show each slide with its speaker notes.
- **Export PDF** downloads a copy to print or share. Restricted documents are watermarked with your name and the date.

## Versions and changes

Every saved change is kept. Open **Version history** to see who changed a document and when, and choose **Compare with previous** to see what changed:

- **Formatted** shows the document as it reads, with removed text struck through in red and added text in green.
- **Source** shows the exact text, line by line.

Some people see only approved versions. Changes still waiting for review are visible to the document's editors and reviewers.
