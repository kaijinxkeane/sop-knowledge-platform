# Reviewing and Approving Changes

Space leads keep SOPs accurate. The **Review** page collects everything waiting for a lead's decision.

## SOP changes

1. Open **Review** and choose **SOP changes**.
2. Select a change to compare it with the approved version. Removed text is struck through in red; added text is green.
3. Choose **Approve this version**, or **Request changes** with a comment for the author.

Approval covers exactly the version you saw. If the author edits it again, it needs a fresh approval. Once approved, the assistant can cite it in answers.

Tick **Material change** when the change matters enough that people must read it again. Everyone who had signed it off as required reading is asked to read the new version.

If you wrote part of the change yourself, approving it is recorded as self-approved.

## AI label suggestions

When the AI suggests a space or sensitivity for a note, it appears under **AI label suggestions**. Check it, adjust it if needed, and approve or reject it. Suggestions can only make a note stricter, never more open.

## Unplaced notes and names

- **Unplaced notes** are notes the system couldn't place in a space. Choose where they belong.
- Notes that mention people's or organisations' names stay restricted until a reviewer confirms the names are safe to show.

## Governance

The **Governance** tab shows SOPs coming up for their regular review, so they don't go stale.
