# Access Levels and Spaces

Every document belongs to a **space**, usually a department such as core delivery, IT or HR. Inside its space it has one of three **sensitivity levels**.

## The three levels

- **Public**: all staff, in every department.
- **Internal**: the people in that department, including its lead.
- **Restricted**: selected people only, usually the department's lead and anyone given lead-level access.

Your role decides how far you can read in each space. For example, a trainer can usually read public documents everywhere and internal documents in their own department.

## Rules that keep documents safe

- **When in doubt, restricted.** A document with a missing or misspelt level is treated as restricted.
- **Tightening is free, loosening needs the lead.** Anyone who can edit a document can make it stricter. Making it more open needs the space lead's approval.
- **The AI only ever tightens.** It can suggest labels, but it never makes a document more open.
- **Safeguards.** Documents that look like they contain personal details, people's names or commercial terms stay restricted until someone checks them.
- **Hidden looks missing.** If you can't open a document, it looks exactly as if it doesn't exist.

> [!note] Need access?
> Ask your space lead. Administrators change roles; for safety, an administrator can't give extra roles to themselves.
