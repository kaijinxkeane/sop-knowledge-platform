**How this assistant uses your information.** Your questions, the assistant's
answers and your Library searches are stored for **24 months** so the company
can meet its yearly audit and improve its SOPs. Your conversation history can be
viewed by you and by the platform administrator; every such view is logged.
Your searches are used to show *you* "gaps from your searches" (topics no SOP
you can access covers yet); nobody else sees them. Space leads see questions
the SOPs couldn't answer, without names, so they can write the missing SOPs.
HR sees only **anonymised, combined** reports about topics where SOPs or
training are missing, and only once at least three people asked; never who
asked what. Handover notes you write are visible only to you, the people you
hand over to, your space's lead and the CEO. Parts of your question and
relevant SOP passages are sent to an AI service to generate answers. Please
don't enter personal data about clients or colleagues unless needed.
Questions? Contact [admin contact].
