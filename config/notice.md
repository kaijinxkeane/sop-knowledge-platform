**How this assistant uses your information.** Your questions and the
assistant's answers are stored for **24 months** so the company can meet its
yearly audit and improve its SOPs. Your conversation history can be viewed by
you and by the platform administrator; every such view is logged. HR sees only
**anonymised, combined** reports about topics where SOPs or training are
missing; never who asked what. Parts of your question and relevant SOP
passages are sent to an AI service to generate answers. Please don't enter
personal data about clients or colleagues unless needed. Questions? Contact
[admin contact].
