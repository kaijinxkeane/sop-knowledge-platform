# Asking Questions

Use **Ask** to get answers from the company's SOPs in plain language.

## How answers work

- Answers come only from **approved** SOPs that you are allowed to read. Drafts and changes still waiting for review are never used.
- Every answer shows its **sources**. Select a source to open the SOP at the section the answer came from.
- If the SOPs don't cover your question, the assistant says so rather than guessing.
- The AI runs on the company's own computer. Your questions are not sent to any outside service.

## Getting good answers

1. Ask one thing at a time, in your own words.
2. Mention the programme or process if it matters, for example "the discovery workshop".
3. Use the suggested follow-up questions under an answer to dig deeper.

## When an answer isn't right

- Choose **Not helpful** so the space lead can see where the SOPs need work.
- Choose **Request an SOP** if nothing covers the topic. The request goes to the space lead.

## Your conversations

Your past conversations are listed on the left. **Hide** removes one from your history. As the staff notice explains, a copy is kept for audit for 24 months and then deleted.
