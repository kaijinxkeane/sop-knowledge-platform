# Handover Notes and Keeping Knowledge

## Handover notes

When you hand work over, for example before leave or when changing roles, write a handover note.

1. Open **Handovers** and choose **New handover**.
2. Write what the other person needs to know and choose who receives it.
3. Send it. Receivers see it under **Awaiting my acknowledgement**.

Only the author, the receivers and the people responsible for them can read a handover note. Receivers confirm with **I have read and understood this handover**.

## Retain (leads and HR)

The **Retain** page helps make sure knowledge doesn't walk out of the door:

- **Gaps**: topics people ask about that the SOPs don't answer well. HR sees totals only when at least three people asked, and never who asked.
- **Coverage**: which programme stages and topics have SOPs.
- **Review due**: SOPs due for their regular review.
- **Required reading**: who has signed off what.
- **Bus factor**: SOPs that only one person has ever written, and staff who are leaving, so a second person can get involved in time.
- **Conflicts**: SOPs that may contradict each other.
- **Handovers**: handover notes and whether they were acknowledged.
