# Required Reading and Onboarding Paths

## Required reading

A space lead can make an approved SOP required reading for a person or for everyone with a role.

- Required reading appears on your **Home** page with its due date.
- Open the SOP, read it and choose **I have read version** to sign it off.
- If the SOP later has a material change, you are asked to read the new version.

Leads assign reading from an approved SOP with **Assign reading**, and follow progress under **Retain**, **Required reading**, where the list can be exported as a spreadsheet.

## Onboarding paths

An onboarding path is a step-by-step plan for someone new, for example a first fortnight for a new trainer. Each step holds SOPs, decks and worksheets.

- Your paths appear on **Home**, with your progress and the next item. Select a path to open it.
- Choose **Done** on an item when you have finished it.
- Items marked **Required** are SOPs you sign off as read instead; they count as done once you have signed off the current version.
- If a path contains something you can't open, it is counted but its title isn't shown.

## Building a path (leads)

1. Open **Retain**, choose **Onboarding paths**, then **New path**.
2. Name it, add steps, and add SOPs, decks or worksheets to each step.
3. Tick **Required** for SOPs people must sign off. Only approved SOPs can be required.
4. Save, then assign it to people or to a role, with a due date if you like.

HR can assign paths and see who has finished them.
